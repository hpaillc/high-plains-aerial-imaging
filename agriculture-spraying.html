Drone Spraying

When a field is too wet, steep or hard to reach with ground equipment, aerial application can be a practical option. We will talk through the job before we fly.

Crop Health Imaging

Field Mapping

Create aerial maps and visual records to support field planning, documentation and operational decisions.

Prescription Application

Support variable-rate or targeted application workflows when compatible prescription data and equipment are provided.

Livestock Visibility

Thermal Monitoring

Thermal imaging can help identify temperature differences and point out areas that may need a closer look.

Columbian Ground Squirrel Control

Ground squirrels can do real damage to pasture, hay ground and equipment areas. For larger or hard-to-reach acreage, aerial application may be a practical fit after we review the property, timing and conditions.
