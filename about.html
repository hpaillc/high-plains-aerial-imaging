ABOUT HIGH PLAINS AERIAL IMAGING

Wyoming is home. I believe in showing up.

I started High Plains Aerial Imaging because I enjoy helping people get a clear look at what they are dealing with—whether that is a field, a job site, a ranch or a piece of equipment.

I served in the Navy aboard CV-43 with VA-55 as an AMH aircraft hydraulics mechanic. The Navy taught me to come prepared, pay attention to details and take responsibility for my work.

After the Navy, I worked construction in Alaska as a journeyman electrician. I also owned a trucking company and managed projects and operations. I know how important it is to communicate clearly, make a plan and follow through.

I was born in Iowa and raised in Wyoming. I spent my summers on my grandparents' farm, and that gave me a lasting respect for the agriculture community and the people who make their living on the land.

Today, I provide insured, FAA Part 107-certified drone imaging, inspection, mapping and agricultural services across Natrona, Converse, Niobrara, Carbon, Albany, Platte, Goshen, Laramie and Fremont Counties. I also hold a Commercial Pesticide Applicator Certificate. If your job is outside that area, give me a call and we will talk it through.
