ABOUT HIGH PLAINS AERIAL IMAGING

Wyoming raised. Built on showing up.

High Plains Aerial Imaging is owned by a Navy veteran who served aboard CV-43 with VA-55 as an electrician. The Navy taught me to be prepared, do the job right and take responsibility for the result.

After the Navy, I worked in construction, owned a trucking company, and led projects and operations. That background taught me the value of clear communication, solid planning and following through.

I was born in Iowa, raised in Wyoming, and spent four summers on my grandparents' farm. That is where my respect for the agriculture community comes from. People who work the land deserve service that is straightforward, dependable and built around the real demands of the day.

Today, we provide insured, FAA Part 107-certified drone imaging, inspection, mapping and agricultural services across Natrona, Converse, Niobrara, Carbon, Albany, Platte, Goshen, Laramie and Fremont Counties. If your job is outside that area, give us a call and we will talk it through.
