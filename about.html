ABOUT HIGH PLAINS AERIAL IMAGING

We help you get a better look at the job.

High Plains Aerial Imaging is an insured, FAA Part 107-certified Wyoming drone business. We take on imaging, inspection, mapping and agricultural jobs for people who need a better look at their property or operation.

We regularly serve Natrona, Converse, Niobrara, Carbon, Albany, Platte, Goshen, Laramie and Fremont Counties. If your job is outside that area, give us a call and we will talk it through.

Every job starts with a simple conversation: What are you trying to see? What would help you make the next decision? Then we plan the flight around that.
